The ``<thm>`` keys: the table
==============================

The engine reads every key of the ``<thm>`` section by its row of one table: the global options, and the keys of an ``experiment[<name>]`` line.  ``pyazr.kinds()["thm"]`` returns the same rows as two record kinds; ``Project.set_thm`` and ``Project.set_thm_experiment`` write them, and the engine refuses a key or value the table does not take.  The physics of each key: :doc:`../theory/thm_implementation`.

<thm> options
-------------

The model options of every THM experiment of the project.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 18 30 12 40

   * - key
     - value
     - default
     - meaning
   * - ``vertex``
     - ``perlevel`` | ``constant`` | ``onshell``
     - ``constant``
     - boundary in the entrance vertex (B - 1) j_l - rho j_l': perlevel S_c(E_lambda) of each level under Brune (alias real); constant B_c = S_c(E_1) of the lowest level of the J group; onshell L_c(E) = S_c(E) + i P_c(E)
   * - ``kinematics``
     - ``lacognata`` | ``triple`` | ``kf3body`` | ``lambda32``
     - ``lacognata``
     - the kinematic factors the model carries, by how the HOES data were extracted: lacognata exit k_f/mu_f (La Cognata's working formula); triple exit Gamma_f alone; kf3body exit Gamma_f/(mu_f k_f); lambda32 exit Gamma_f, entrance 1/k_i on shell
   * - ``entranceL``
     - ``incoherent`` | ``coherent``
     - ``incoherent``
     - entrance partial waves of one channel spin summed coherently or incoherently (for an observable integrated over the exit direction the l cross terms vanish)
   * - ``coulombIntegral``
     - ``0`` | ``1``
     - ``0``
     - add the external Coulomb term 2 eta k int_a^inf O_l(kr)/O_l(ka) j_l(pr) dr to the vertex (Tribble eq. 2.79)
   * - ``spectatorEnergy``
     - number (MeV)
     - ``0``
     - mean spectator kinetic energy <p_sx^2>/2mu_sx added to E + B in the half-off-shell momentum, for every THM pair (one pair: the key spectatorEnergy[<pair key>])

Built-in nuclei
---------------

A nucleus of ``beam=``, ``target=`` or ``spectator=`` is one of these names (nuclear masses of AME2020, ``pyazr.kinds()["nuclides"]``) or ``Z,A,mass`` with the nuclear mass in u: ``n``, ``p``, ``d``, ``t``, ``3He``, ``4He``, ``6Li``, ``7Li``, ``9Be``, ``10B``, ``11B``, ``12C``, ``13C``, ``14N``, ``15N``, ``16O``, ``17O``, ``18O``, ``19F``, ``20Ne``, ``23Na``, ``24Mg``.
